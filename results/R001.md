# R001 — The Cyclotomic Totient Oracle

**Status:** Informally proven  
**Verification status:** Computationally checked by the independent verifier in this repository  
**Lean status:** Not formalized in Lean

## Statement

For prime exponent `p` and `N = 2^p - 1`, the published result identifies the residue of the cyclotomic value `Φ_N(2)` modulo `N²` with a coefficient determined by Euler's totient:

`Φ_N(2) ≡ 1 + (2 φ(N) / p) N (mod N²)`.

The accompanying no-wrap bound makes the coefficient recoverable exactly from the residue.
