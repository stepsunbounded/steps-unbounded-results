# R006 — Private points and the solvable reduction

**Status:** Informally proven / conditional  
**Formal status:** multiple reductions and ascension components are Lean verified; the arbitrary-solvable hypothesis remains open

## Statement

The formal development proves that private-point and linear-independence hypotheses imply Herzog–Schönheim, and that a suitable `LI_p` hypothesis for finite solvable groups would imply the solvable case.

It does **not** prove `LI_p` (equivalently the required `H_p` package) for every finite solvable group. The remaining gap is represented explicitly in the Lean development rather than assumed silently.
