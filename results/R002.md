# R002 — The Fermat Quotient as a Dilation-Correlation Defect

**Status:** Informally proven  
**Formal status:** Lean verified for the counting form, subject to this repository's CI and axiom audit

## Statement

For a prime `r > 3`, positive integer `c`, and the block-harmonic autocorrelations defined in the accompanying write-up, the doubling defect is the base-2 Fermat quotient:

`R(2) - R(1) = -c · q_r(2)` in `ZMod r`.

When `r ∤ c`, equality of the two autocorrelations is equivalent to the base-2 Wieferich condition.

The Lean development formalizes the counting form. The interpretation as a correlation over the quotient group for prime `P = 2cr - 1` remains part of the mathematical presentation rather than the formal statement.
