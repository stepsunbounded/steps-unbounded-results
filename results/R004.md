# R004 — Lifting through a normal p-subgroup over A₅

**Status:** Lean verified, subject to this repository's CI and axiom audit

## Statement

Let `G` be a finite group and `V ⊴ G` a normal `p`-subgroup. If `G/V ≃ A₅`, then `G` satisfies the Herzog–Schönheim property.

The formal development proves the lifting theorem from `LI_p(G/V)` and separately proves `LI_p(A₅)` internally from the private-point/capacity argument. The final A₅ extension theorem is therefore unconditional.
