# R005 — Homogeneous composition factors

**Status:** Informally proven  
**Formal status:** structural mechanism and the A₅ instance are Lean verified; the classification-wide per-simple-group data layer is not fully formalized

## Statement

For a finite group whose composition factors are all isomorphic to a fixed finite simple group `S`, the formal development reduces Herzog–Schönheim to explicit subgroup-index data for `S` and a reciprocal-mass bound for the multiplicative monoid generated by those indices.

The Lean development proves the extension/composition-factor mechanism, the uniform Euler-product criterion, and the unconditional `A₅` instance. It does **not** claim that all classification-wide index inputs have been formalized.
