# R003 — Direct powers of A₅ satisfy Herzog–Schönheim

**Status:** Lean verified, subject to this repository's CI and axiom audit

## Statement

The formal development proves the Herzog–Schönheim property for direct powers of `A₅`, together with the associated product/quotient extensions represented by the audited headline theorems below.

The proof uses the subgroup-index mass criterion and an explicit bound for the multiplicative monoid generated by the subgroup indices of `A₅`.
